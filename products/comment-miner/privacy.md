# Comment Miner privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Comment Miner is a browser extension that exports the public comments of a YouTube video you are viewing to a CSV or JSON file.

**What the extension reads.** When you click "Collect comments", it asks YouTube for the comments of the video open in your tab, the same way the comments section under the video loads them, and reads the video title and channel name. For each comment it keeps the public author name and channel link, the text, like and reply counts, the date YouTube shows, and whether the comment is pinned, edited or hearted by the creator. These requests are made without your YouTube sign-in, so nothing tied to your account is read or used. It does nothing on other websites.

**Where your data goes.** The comments are kept only in your own browser (extension storage) until you export, copy or clear them. Exports are created on your computer. The extension has no servers of its own and no analytics, and it never sends the comments anywhere.

**License checks.** If you buy Pro, the license key you enter is sent to our payment provider (Gumroad, Polar or Lemon Squeezy) to confirm it is valid, and re-checked about once a week. In Firefox, you are asked for permission before the key is sent. Nothing else is sent with it. The payment provider's privacy policy covers your purchase details.

**No sale or sharing.** We do not sell, rent or share any data. We do not use data for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than running the extension.

**Your control.** Click "Clear collected comments" in the extension to delete the saved comments. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data. You are responsible for how you use the comments you export, including respecting YouTube's terms and the privacy of the people who wrote them.

**Contact.** Questions can be sent through the support link on the extension's store listing.
